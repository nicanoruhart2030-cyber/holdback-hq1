Same dance floor.

What I play

80s, 90s and today’s hits.

Mixed for every crowd.

  1. 80s

    The ones you danced to

    Synths, new wave, disco, rock en español. Every summer on the coast.

  2. 90s

    The ones you sang

    Dance, pop and rock, in Spanish and English. Hands up, everybody singing.

  3. Now

    The ones your kids ask for

    Today’s hits too, mixed in at the right moment. Nobody sits down.

Forty years of reading dance floors. I watch who’s there and what moves them, and I play to that crowd, so it’s a night you’ll remember.
